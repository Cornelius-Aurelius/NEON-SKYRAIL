PILOT BRIEFING

HOW TO FLY

Stay on the Skyrail for as long as possible. Shift between three lanes to avoid red barriers. Every obstacle row leaves at least one safe lane.

Desktop: Arrow keys or A / D to steer. Space to activate boost. P or Escape to pause.

Phone / tablet: Use large movement buttons or swipe left/right across the track. Tap Boost when the energy meter fills.

Collect cyan flux diamonds to power your boost. Survive with three shields. Endless offers unlimited distance, Rush has a 90-second clock, and Training lets you practise without losing.
